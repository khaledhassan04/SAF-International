OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

Precision Global Sourcing with Boutique Craft Care

SAF International is a specialized trading and sourcing corporation engineered to eliminate the uncertainties of cross-border procurement for discerning organizations.

Whether fulfilling custom-molded conference hardware or hand-thrown ceramics, our end-to-end oversight guarantees exact conformity to international compliance standards.
